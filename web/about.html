I’m a backend-focused software programmer with more than a couple of years of experience in Java, Kotlin, and Spring Boot. I care about TDD/BDD and good practices, and about integrating AI into that workflow responsibly. This blog helped me refresh concepts I hadn’t touched in years — I wish someone had explained things this simply when I was starting out, so I decided to share it with the world in case it helps someone else.

Outside of the blog, I maintain the Open Football Project (footballproject.org), a football analytics platform built around a Kotlin/Spring Boot API, a live-events service, a React web app, and a React Native mobile app. It aggregates and normalises football leagues, scores, and stats from around the world, and it’s open source.
